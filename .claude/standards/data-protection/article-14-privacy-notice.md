## Privacy Notice: How KMS Compliance Ltd Uses Your Business Contact Information

**Last updated:** [DATE]

This notice explains how KMS Compliance Ltd ("KMS", "we", "us")
collects and uses your personal data if you have received a marketing
communication from us and we did not obtain your details directly from
you. It is provided in accordance with Article 14 of the UK General
Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR).

### 1. Who we are
KMS Compliance Ltd is a UK-based FCA compliance consultancy supporting
sole traders and small, founder-led financial services firms.
- Registered office: [ADDRESS]
- Company number: [NUMBER]
- Contact: [EMAIL] / [PHONE: 07368 387972]
- ICO registration number: [ICO NUMBER]

### 2. What data we hold and where it came from
We hold the following categories of business contact data about you:
your name, job title or role, business email address, business phone
number, company name, company registration number, registered or
trading address, and industry/sector classification (SIC code).

This information was obtained from **Companies House**, the UK's public
register of companies, which publishes this data as part of its
statutory transparency function.

### 3. Why we process your data and our legal basis
We use your business contact details to send you information about FCA
authorisation and compliance support services relevant to your sector,
specifically our FCA Launch Pad service for firms that may require FCA
authorisation.

Our legal basis for this processing is **legitimate interests**
(Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR). We have carried out a documented Legitimate
Interests Assessment (`outreach-lia.md`) weighing our commercial
interest in reaching relevant firms against your rights and interests,
and concluded that our interest does not override yours, provided you
retain a straightforward right to object at any time (see Section 7).

We do not process any special category data about you, and we do not
use your data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces
legal or similarly significant effects.

### 4. Who we share it with
We share limited data with trusted processors who act only on our
instructions and under a data processing agreement, specifically our
mailing/fulfilment providers (e.g. Docmail/Intelliprint) for direct
mail **[CONFIRM: only include this if direct mail is actually in use —
see the open issue in `outreach-lia.md`]**, and our CRM provider
(Notion) for lead management. We do not sell your data or share it with
unrelated third parties for their own marketing purposes.

### 6. How long we keep your data
We retain business contact data obtained for marketing purposes for
[X months/years, e.g. 12 months] from the point of last engagement or
from collection, whichever is later. If you object or opt out (see
below), we suppress your details from future marketing immediately and
retain only the minimum record needed to ensure we do not contact you
again.

### 7. Your rights
Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:
- **Object** to this processing at any time, including the right to
  stop direct marketing immediately and without needing to give a
  reason (Article 21). Every marketing communication we send includes a
  one-click opt-out.
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectify** inaccurate data.
- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten") in certain
  circumstances.
- **Restrict** processing in certain circumstances.
- **Complain** to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you
  believe we have not handled your data lawfully — ico.org.uk, or
  0303 123 1113.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [PRIVACY/DPO EMAIL].

### 8. No obligation to provide data
You are not contractually or statutorily required to provide any data
to us, and there are no consequences for you if you choose not to
engage — you can simply opt out.
