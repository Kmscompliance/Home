HOWTO: statement
NOTE: **How the options work in this document.** A Statement of Responsibilities depends on your firm’s **SM&CR category**, not on your regulated activities, so the options in this document are by category: **Limited Scope** or **Core**. Keep the option for your firm’s category in sections 3 and 4 and delete the other. (This template does not cover **Enhanced** firms, which have additional requirements such as a Management Responsibilities Map and “overall responsibility” for every business area.)
NOTE: **Which category is my firm?** In broad terms: firms whose only regulated activities are consumer credit activities under a **limited permission** (for example credit broking as a secondary activity, or not-for-profit debt advice) are usually **Limited Scope**, as are sole traders and some other specialist firms. Most other solo-regulated firms – including full-permission consumer credit firms, insurance intermediaries whose principal business is insurance distribution, mortgage intermediaries and investment advisers – are **Core**. **Payment institutions, e-money institutions and registered account information service providers are not within SM&CR at all** and do not need this document. Check your category against the FCA’s SM&CR categorisation guidance and SUP 10C before completing it.
NOTE: **What this document is for.** Every person performing a Senior Management Function (SMF) must have a Statement of Responsibilities (SoR) setting out clearly and succinctly what they are responsible for. The FCA’s own SoR form is submitted through FCA Connect with the individual’s application for approval (Form A) and whenever the individual’s responsibilities change significantly. Use this template to prepare and agree the content internally, keep it as your firm’s record, and then transfer the content to the FCA’s form. Since PS26/6 (April 2026), firms must submit applications within 12 weeks of the need arising, and have more time to submit updated SoRs after changes – check SUP 10C for the current deadlines.
WATCH: HM Treasury’s April 2026 response on SM&CR reform includes legislative changes (through the Financial Services and Markets Bill) that would let regulators replace pre-approval with notification for some senior manager roles and remove the Certification Regime from legislation. The FCA expects to consult on “phase 2” changes, which may affect SoRs, once the legislation is in place. Check the current position before submitting.

#! Guidance on writing a good Statement of Responsibilities
NOTE: Delete this section before submitting.
- **Be clear and specific.** The FCA and the individual must be able to tell, from the statement alone, exactly what the individual is accountable for. Avoid vague words such as “supporting”, “assisting” or “involved in”.
- **Be succinct.** Describe responsibilities, not day-to-day tasks or job descriptions.
- **Be consistent.** The responsibilities must match your governance arrangements, organisation chart, policies and business plan. If you are a Core firm, every Prescribed Responsibility must be allocated to someone.
- **Avoid gaps and unnecessary overlaps.** Every key area of the business should be the responsibility of someone. Only share responsibilities where that genuinely reflects how the firm is run.
- **Keep it up to date.** Review each statement at least annually and whenever responsibilities change. Submit a revised statement to the FCA when there is a significant change, within the time allowed by SUP 10C.
