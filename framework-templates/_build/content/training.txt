# 1. Introduction and purpose
This Training and Competence Policy sets out how [Insert firm legal name] (“we”, “the firm”) makes sure that everyone who works for us – directors, employees, temporary staff and contractors [and appointed representatives – delete if you have none] – has, and keeps, the knowledge, skills and behaviours needed to do their job competently, ethically and in line with our regulatory obligations.
Our regulated activities are: [Specify the regulated activities / permissions your firm holds or is applying for].
This policy is designed to meet:
- the **competent employees rule** (SYSC 5.1.1R), which applies to every firm and requires us to employ personnel with the skills, knowledge and expertise necessary for the responsibilities allocated to them;
- the **Training and Competence sourcebook (TC)**, where it applies to our activities (see section 7);
- the **Senior Managers and Certification Regime** and the **Conduct Rules** (COCON), where they apply to us;
- the **Consumer Duty**, which requires us to make sure staff understand how their role affects customer outcomes; and
- the training requirements in our other policies, including anti-money laundering (regulation 24 of the Money Laundering Regulations 2017, where they apply), data protection, customer vulnerability, complaints and financial promotions.
**Policy objectives.** This policy aims to: set minimum training standards for each role; make sure no one carries out a regulated or customer-facing activity until they are competent to do so (or are properly supervised); measure, record and maintain competence; and build a culture of continuous learning and good customer outcomes.

# 2. Scope
This policy applies to everyone acting on our behalf, whatever their contract or seniority, including anyone who:
- deals with customers or potential customers;
- carries out, supervises or oversees a regulated activity;
- designs, approves or communicates financial promotions;
- carries out customer due diligence or handles payments;
- handles personal data or complaints; or
- performs a senior management or certification function.

**3.4 All staff.** Complete required training on time; apply what they learn; ask for help when unsure; and tell their manager if they believe they need more training.

# 4. Training programme
**4.1 Induction.** Everyone completes induction training before they carry out any customer-facing or regulated activity. Induction covers: our business, permissions and customers; the FCA’s Principles, the Consumer Duty and (where applicable) the Conduct Rules; our key policies (anti-money laundering and financial crime, customer due diligence, data protection, customer vulnerability, complaints and financial promotions); role-specific procedures; and information security.
**4.2 Role-specific training.** Staff complete the training in section 2 and section 7 for their role before they work unsupervised.
**4.3 Annual refresher training.** Everyone completes refresher training at least annually on anti-money laundering and financial crime, data protection, the Consumer Duty and customer vulnerability, and on the rules for their role.
**4.4 Regulatory change and targeted training.** We provide additional training when rules or our procedures change, and when monitoring, complaints, incidents or performance reviews show a gap.
**4.5 Development.** We support staff to develop further, for example through professional qualifications or supervisory training.
**4.6 Delivery methods.** Training is delivered through [Insert methods, e.g. e-learning, face-to-face sessions, shadowing, coaching, policy walkthroughs and case studies]. Each module ends with a knowledge check or declaration of understanding.

# 5. Assessing and maintaining competence
**5.1 Before working unsupervised.** Before anyone carries out a regulated or customer-facing activity without supervision, they must: complete induction and role-specific training; pass any required knowledge assessments; attain any qualification the rules require for their activity (section 7); and be assessed as competent by [Insert name or role] through observation or file review.
**5.2 Supervision.** Until assessed as competent, staff work under supervision. The level of supervision depends on their experience and the risk of the activity [Insert, e.g. 100% file checking for the first X cases].
**5.3 Ongoing competence.** We check competence at least [Insert frequency, e.g. quarterly] using file reviews, call or conversation monitoring, customer outcome data (for example complaints and errors) and one-to-one reviews.
**5.4 When competence falls short.** Where competence gaps are identified, we may increase supervision, provide additional training or coaching, restrict the activities the person can carry out, or – where appropriate – use our disciplinary procedures. We consider whether any customers were affected and need to be contacted.

# 10. Failure to complete training or maintain competence
Missing mandatory training, failing assessments, or falling below required competence standards is taken seriously. Depending on the circumstances, we may set new deadlines, provide extra support, increase supervision, restrict the person from certain activities until the issue is resolved, or take disciplinary action in line with our HR procedures. For SM&CR firms, persistent failure may affect a person’s fitness and propriety assessment.
