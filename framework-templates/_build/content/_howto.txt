#! How to use this template
NOTE: Read this page first, then delete it (and every other shaded “KMS guidance” box) before you submit or adopt the document. Nothing in this section forms part of your {DOC}.
This template is written for firms applying for, or holding, FCA authorisation. It contains universal wording that applies to every firm, plus **activity-specific options** for the parts where the rules genuinely differ between regulated activities.
- **Yellow highlighted text in square brackets** is a placeholder or an instruction. Replace every placeholder with your firm’s own details and remove the brackets. Before you finish, search the document for “[” to make sure none are left.
- **Green “START OF OPTION” and “END OF OPTION” banners** mark self-contained option blocks. Keep only the option(s) that match the permissions your firm holds or is applying for. Delete every other option in full – from its START banner to its END banner inclusive – and then delete the banners of the options you keep.
- Each option is written to stand alone. It does not rely on wording, numbering or definitions in any other option, so deleting one never requires you to edit another. Options are labelled with the section number and a letter (for example 4A, 4C). Gaps in the lettering after you delete options are expected and do not need to be renumbered.
- **Supporting registers are mandatory.** This document relies on the KMS registers listed in its “Supporting registers” section (where it has one). The FCA judges a firm on the records that show its policies working in practice, so a firm that adopts this document without using its registers is likely to fail FCA scrutiny. Start using them from day one.
- **Grey “KMS guidance” boxes** explain what a section needs and how to complete it. Delete them once you have acted on them.
- **Amber “Regulatory watch” boxes** (also flagged with a Word comment) mark points that depend on FCA or Government material that is still in consultation, under legal challenge or not yet in force. Check the current position before you submit, then delete the box.
- If your firm holds several permissions (for example credit broking and insurance distribution), keep every option that applies. Where two kept options contain overlapping wording, that is intentional – each must make sense on its own.
- When you have finished, update the contents list if the document has one (right-click it and choose “Update Field”).
NOTE: Your {DOC} must describe what your firm will actually do. The FCA will test it against your business plan, systems, staff and resources. Remove or adapt any commitment you cannot meet in practice – an over-promising document is a weakness in an application, not a strength.
